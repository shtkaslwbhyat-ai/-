# Thumbnail Prompt Template — ORIGIN EMPIRE

**Goal (الهدف):** أن تبدو كل صورة مصغّرة كأنها ملصق لفيلم وثائقي عالمي عن المال
والأعمال: فخمة، سينمائية، واضحة، وتثير الفضول من النظرة الأولى.

Usage notes:
- Fill the four bracketed fields from the verified research (Stage A). The
  "dramatic story moment" must be factually accurate and covered in the video.
- The image is generated **without any text**. The Arabic headline (2–4 words,
  IBM Plex Sans Arabic, bold) is added afterwards in the editor, in the negative
  space the prompt reserves.
- Real founders: never a recognizable face. Use a silhouette, rear view,
  hands/objects, or a stylized non-identifiable figure (owner's decision).
- Generate 2–3 variants and test them at mobile size (~168×94 px) before choosing.

---

```
Create a premium, high-CTR YouTube documentary thumbnail for the Arabic business documentary channel ORIGIN EMPIRE.
FORMAT: 16:9 widescreen, 1280x720, ultra-sharp, professional cinematic composition.
EPISODE SUBJECT: [COMPANY / FOUNDER / BUSINESS STORY]
MAIN VISUAL: [ONE powerful, recognizable subject or object]
DRAMATIC STORY MOMENT: [THE MOST INTERESTING FACTUALLY ACCURATE MOMENT FROM THE STORY]
VISUAL STORYTELLING: Communicate the central conflict or mystery through one instantly understandable visual metaphor.
VISUAL STYLE: Premium semi-realistic 3D documentary realism, natural human proportions, realistic facial anatomy, highly detailed materials, authentic historical environments, sophisticated cinematic production design.
BRAND COLORS:

* Midnight Navy #101A2A
* Empire Gold #C49A48
* Ivory #E8E5DD
* Steel Blue #526579
* Deep Charcoal #171B22

COMPOSITION:

* One dominant focal point.
* Main subject positioned on the left or right third.
* Clear negative space on the opposite side for Arabic headline.
* Strong foreground-background separation.
* Dramatic but realistic lighting.
* Restrained gold highlights.
* Deep cinematic shadows.
* Visually simple and immediately understandable.

EMOTIONAL IMPACT: Create curiosity, tension, surprise, ambition, or a sense of mystery, according to the actual story.
IMPORTANT RESTRICTIONS:

* No generated text or random letters.
* No fake company logos.
* No misleading historical scenes.
* No childish Pixar style.
* No cartoon proportions.
* No plastic-looking faces.
* No cluttered collage.
* No unnecessary arrows or circles.
* No watermark.
* Do not add decorative elements that distract from the main subject.

The result must look like an expensive international business-history documentary poster, instantly recognizable at small mobile thumbnail size.
```
